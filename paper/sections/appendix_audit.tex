\subsection*{F.4\quad Claims we could not trace, and checks that misreported}

\textbf{One prose-versus-constants gap is left open rather than reconciled.} The ingest record
describes the normalization warm-up as ``the $\sim$800-bar z-score warm-up''. The pinned constants
give $180$ bars for the fast z-score, $720$ for the slow and $1{,}440$ for the volatility scale.
We could not reconcile the description with the constants, so \cref{sec:data-causal} quotes only
the \emph{measured} drop --- $809$ bars of $279{,}930$ on the thinnest instrument --- and a source
comment records the unreconciled description. No number in the paper depends on the
reconciliation.

\textbf{Two verification checks misreported during this pass, and both were the check's fault.} A
bibliography-versus-PDF checker reported an entry missing; the entry renders correctly and the
checker's surname extraction had broken on a \LaTeX{} accent escape. And a watermark check
reported Fig 8 unwatermarked; the watermark is present and legible, and \texttt{pdftotext}
fragments rotated glyphs in that figure so the string match failed. Both were resolved by looking
at the artifact rather than by trusting the probe, and neither was reported as a finding. They are
recorded here because a probe's own defect presenting as a finding is the failure mode this
project has hit most often.

\textbf{One layout defect was found by looking at a render rather than by any check:} Fig 8's
outcome box overran its panel at the original font size. Fixed and re-verified visually.
