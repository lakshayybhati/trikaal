\begin{abstract}
Two-stage models for financial time series learn a tokenizer under a reconstruction objective and
then fit an autoregressive model to the resulting discrete codes. This design carries an unstated
assumption: that a representation good enough to reconstruct from is good enough to forecast from.
We show that it fails in a specific, predictable direction. Reconstruction objectives allocate code
capacity by variance and covariance and never by downstream value, so wherever the budget is tight
and the competing dimensions are correlated enough to amortize one another, a low-variance channel
that is weakly covariant with price is excluded deterministically rather than by chance. On an
entropy-calibrated fixture, per-dimension reconstruction sorts by variance class --- $0.98$ for the
high-variance return channel, $0.82$--$0.92$ for the mutually correlated block, and
$0.001$--$0.014$ for the one independent dimension --- and a signal worth $1.151$ nats planted in
feature space is not recovered at all, while $0.900$ nats planted directly in token space is
recovered by the identical backbone at $94.4\%$, localizing the loss to the tokenizer--backbone
interface. We re-specify that interface so the fine subtoken is a per-bar encoding of the current
bar. \textbf{That change alone is necessary and not sufficient}: it leaves per-bar legibility at
chance on the fixture --- $0.4995$--$0.5182$ across three seeds against a before-value of
$0.5112$ --- and only a microstructure class weight, fitted on that same fixture and introduced in
response to that failure, carries it to a seed mean of $0.9047$ whose minimum is $0.8839$, at
unchanged bits per token. We convert that property into a pre-registered hard stop on real data.
\textbf{On $304{,}625{,}181$ one-minute bars across $200$ instruments, that gate fired.} The
re-specified tokenizer does not make microstructure per-bar legible on real market data, and the
failure localizes: $97.3\%$ of the shortfall falls on trade-flow imbalance and signed count
imbalance --- the two \emph{signed} channels --- while the four magnitude channels essentially
pass, and under a shuffled-microstructure placebo the signed channels collapse toward their base
rates while the magnitude channels hold. \textbf{The tokenizer preserves the microstructure that
duplicates what price already carries and loses the microstructure that is independent of it.}
A firing was pre-registered as the modal prediction of the mechanism and as the event that makes
the mechanism the primary result; the five-cell ablation it gates was therefore never run, and this
paper reports no information ratio, no null and no verdict. The finding is bounded by one budget
point, one objective and one architecture, and by a class weight fitted on a synthetic proxy: it
establishes that this tokenizer does not carry the signed channels, not that none can.

\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item We identify and mechanistically characterize \emph{capacity eviction}: under a
    reconstruction objective, code capacity is allocated by variance and covariance and never by
    downstream value, so a task-relevant channel that is low-variance and weakly covariant is
    excluded deterministically. We isolate the effect with a fixture in which the evicted
    dimension and the retained ones differ in exactly one property --- statistical independence
    --- and show the exclusion reproduces across seeds (\cref{sec:mechanism}).
  \item We localize the failure by construction rather than by inference: the same backbone at
    the same budget recovers $94.4\%$ of a signal planted in token space and none of a
    larger signal planted in feature space, which acquits model capacity and indicts the
    tokenizer--backbone interface (\cref{sec:mech-localize}).
  \item We introduce a per-bar tokenizer interface --- a pointwise fine subtoken with a
    bottleneck decode leg and calibrated class weighting --- that restores per-bar legibility at
    unchanged bits per token, and a \emph{legibility gate} that enforces the property on real
    data as a pre-second-stage hard stop (\cref{sec:mech-fix,sec:mech-gate}).
  \item We run a pre-registered, capacity-matched five-cell ablation with a shuffled
    microstructure placebo on $304{,}625{,}181$ one-minute bars, scored by cost-aware
    net information ratio under purged walk-forward validation, with the decision rule, seed
    count and multiple-testing correction fixed before any result existed
    (\cref{sec:setup,sec:results}).
\end{itemize}
