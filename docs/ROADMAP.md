# Trikaal v1 — Execution Roadmap

The **design** blueprint is the spec (`docs/specs/2026-06-18-trikaal-v1-design.md`, 9 subsystems). This file is the **build order**: the sequence of milestones from here to a shipped paper, each with a testable exit gate. The builder self-sequences against this — it is the answer to "which way next?".

**Standing discipline (overrides any tempt-to-expand).** v1 is **one** sharp, controlled, honestly-evaluated claim — the microstructure-aware FSQ tokenizer on a **31,795,200-parameter** realized backbone (21,301,248 excluding the MTP heads) — *not* a race to be "the best." No v2/v3 items pulled forward (regime-conditioning, OFI/L2 depth, meta-labeling/sizing, cross-asset, base-class scale). No new "novelty." **Honest over impressive; a clean null is a valid, publishable outcome.** Anything that tempts scope expansion: *parked, we build.*

**Critical path:** M2 ✅ → M3 ✅ → M5 ✅ → M4a ✅ → M4b ✅ (full 200-symbol lake, 304.6 M bars, Merkle `5dfd667d…`) → **M6 ✅ (stopped by its own gate — see below)** → M7 → M8. M4/M5 are *independent* (parallelizable with two builders), but with one builder **M5 runs first** — it is local/cheap and the highest-risk-to-find-late subsystem — and **M4 crosses the cloud boundary**, deferred until just before M6 so we cross into paid compute once, with a proven harness.

**★ Independence framing — CORRECTED 2026-08-17, and the previous wording was false.** This line used to read *"Cell 1 is **our BSQ baseline, externally validated against published Kronos-small**… the public weights appear only inside the eval harness as a validation target."* **No Kronos weights were ever pulled, and none ever will be** — docs/ENGINEERING.md invariant 8 forbids it, and the §8.C.3 external-validation gate was found unexecutable and **dropped as binding** (Lakshay, 2026-08-03; prereg §7 v1.6.22): the paper publishes two Kronos_small RankICs 2.4× apart, both on Shanghai Stock Exchange 15-minute equity bars — a market and frequency v1 firewalls out — and running the weights would need Kronos model code. The correct framing is: **Cell 1 is our own BSQ baseline at matched bits-per-token, and it is NOT externally validated.** The compensating control is a required disclosure carried in every verdict manifest and refused if absent: *"we cannot exclude that our BSQ baseline is weaker than a reference BSQ implementation, which would inflate the FSQ-vs-BSQ comparison reported in §5."* docs/ENGINEERING.md has said this since 2026-08-03; this file and its M5/M6 entries did not, so a reader of the roadmap alone would have believed a validation we did not perform. Corrected in all four places.

## Milestones

**M0 — Blueprint** ✅
- **Goal:** full v1 design (9 subsystems) frozen.
- **Exit gate:** spec adversarially audited (4-lens); docs/ENGINEERING.md invariants locked.
- **Compute:** —
- **De-risk:** nothing is built against assumptions; the spec wins every conflict.

**M1 — Synthetic vertical slice** ✅
- **Goal:** prove the *whole architecture* compiles and is leak-free before downloading a byte.
- **Exit gate:** G0 (exhaustive causal sweep, 100% bar coverage + planted leaks fail), G1 (tokenizer recon MAE < 1e-3; backbone+MTP CE < 0.05), G2 (bit-identical determinism); realized params == 21,301,248.
- **Compute:** local.
- **De-risk:** a wiring/causality bug found here costs minutes, not a multi-TB re-download.

**M2 — Single-symbol real slice (BTCUSDT 2023)** ✅
- **Goal:** prove the pipeline + tokenizer work on *real* signal, and measure whether microstructure carries standalone forward signal.
- **Exit gate:** real ingest→lake (SHA-256, content-hashed manifest), causal exhaustive sweep PASS on real bars (700/700), Stage-1 FSQ converges (val MAE 0.0756, no collapse, Δ_fine alive); per-feature causal IC screen + first-cut MDE delivered.
- **Result:** signed order-flow predicts standalone — TFI |RankIC| 0.027, signed_count_imbalance 0.020 @ h=5 (CI excludes 0); magnitude channels (trade_count/dispersion/large_share) live-no-signal; funding/OI masked. Two-cell temporal MDE 0.0038. **Univariate liveness only — NOT yet incremental-over-OHLCV (that is M6's 2×2 marginal + Cell 5 placebo). |RankIC| ~0.02–0.03 is a thin edge; IC ≠ net-IR after costs.**
- **Compute:** local MPS (dev-grade; *not* a determinism-gated artifact).
- **De-risk:** proves one coin-year before the multi-TB universe; the IC screen is the cheap early read that decides whether M6's microstructure arms are worth GPU-days (→ **M6 Gate B**).

**M3 — Stage-2 AR on the single symbol** ✅
- **Goal:** prove the backbone *learns to predict* real tokens (hierarchical coarse→fine + MTP) and the generation/KV-cache path runs end-to-end.
- **Exit gate:** AR train/val loss converges and **beats a trivial next-token baseline** on BTCUSDT-2023; the KV-cache `step` path is wired and bit-equivalent to the parallel forward; produces a usable single-symbol checkpoint.
- **Result (qualified PASS):** train NLL falls 13.38→11.93 (backbone fits real tokens); best-val (step 250) beats the train-marginal CE — **coarse +0.1195, fine +0.0419 nats** (coarse/regime token predictable, fine/residual near-noise); all 4 MTP depths drop (~0.5 nats each); **KV-cache rollout bit-exact, max|Δ| 3.3e-6**; checkpoint saved + content-hashed (`predictor_hash 6d118ede…`). Honest caveats: modest margin + fast single-coin-year overfit (val min ~step 250) — the expected single-regime limit; top-1 acc near the uniform floor for 891/1225-way stochastic targets (CE is the signal); MPS dev-grade (predictor weights not bit-reproducible). Write-up: `docs/milestone3_stage2_btcusdt.md`.
- **Compute:** local MPS (dev-grade checkpoint — the last cheap single-symbol de-risk).
- **De-risk:** confirms the predictor works before the universe and the 15-cycle ablation; **its checkpoint is the fixture M5 is validated against (→ M6 Gate A).**

**M4 — Full universe ingest + cloud transition** — ⚑ **CLOUD BOUNDARY (VPS kicks in)**
- **Goal:** §2 at scale — the full, delisting-aware universe lake.
- **M4a (LOCAL, no spend)** ✅ — generalized the M2 pipeline to N symbols as a **pure per-symbol fan-out** (`universe.py`, `universe_build.py`, `universe_ingest.py`, `manifest.py`, `reduce_shard.py`, `config/universe.yaml`). **Dry-run on local BTCUSDT-2023 reproduces M2 `dataset_hash` `sha256:1d3ec4f8…` BIT-EXACTLY** (regression green); synthetic 2-symbol fixture (one delisted) runs end-to-end with **per-symbol independence** (a symbol's hash is identical alone vs in-universe) and the exhaustive causal sweep **leak-free (100% coverage)** on each (incl. truncated) stream; resumable content-hashed `(symbol,month)` shard reducer is byte-identical to the in-memory path. Cost estimate: **100 pairs ≈ 90 GB / 9 GB lake / ~1.4 core-h; 200 pairs ≈ 180 GB / 19 GB / ~2.9 core-h (~214 M bars, < $25 one-time, bottleneck = download/storage not CPU, well under the ~1–2 B-bar saturation point)**. Adversarial-reviewed the causal/normalization generalization (no cross-symbol leak path). Write-ups: `docs/milestone4a_universe_ingest.md` + turnkey `docs/milestone4b_runbook.md`. **Tests green + ruff clean.**
- **M4b (LOCAL, parallel + raw-cache)** ✅ — **full universe lake COMPLETE: 200/200 symbols, 304,625,181 bars (2021-01→2025-01), universe Merkle `5dfd667d…`, compacted to 7,024 month-partitioned parquet files (15 GB).** Bootstrapped the survivorship-correct universe FROM THE DUMP INDEX (`universe_bootstrap.py`: S3 scan → 200 USDT perps incl. **41 delisted**, listing/delisting from dump coverage) → `config/universe_full.yaml` + corpus-purpose metadata. Governed producer–consumer (`ingest_orchestrator.py`): SHA-256-verified downloads → bounded raw cache → in-memory reduce → lake → ledger-recorded → **raw EVICTED (evict-after-record, single chokepoint)**; resumable, failure-isolated. Full run: **~52.8 h at ~2.3 MB/s sustained** (the 8.3 MB/s burst didn't hold), peak cache 30 GB, 503 GB evicted → raw 0; AXSUSDT failed on a transient timeout, **recovered by resume → 200/200**. **In-line causal sweep GREEN at scale as a HARD GATE on both classes** (`[live] HOOKUSDT`, `[tail] YFIIUSDT`, `[live] AXSUSDT`; RED → `FatalIngestError` aborts before record/evict). 304.6 M realized (below the 500 M–1 B band; adequate — eval/regime breadth, not training fuel — `[[training-saturation-budget]]`). Data quality: **99.56 % OHLCV-usable, 98.58 % +micro-usable, 99.0 % micro-availability, 0 micro-starved coins**, 43 thin-by-bar-count coins (`docs/_m4b_dq_table.md`). Lake compaction proven **hash-preserving** (all 200 dataset_hashes re-derived == Merkle `5dfd667d…`, 0 mismatches); manifest regenerated at 200 via a single-GROUP-BY rollup (`--manifest-only`). 14-agent + supervisor adversarial reviews (2 HIGH + 1 MED found+fixed; sweep hard-gated live+tail; orchestrator-path independence test). Write-up: `docs/milestone4b_universe_ingest.md`.
- **Exit gate:** ✅ **MET** — 200 USDT pairs ingested (SHA-256, content-hashed manifest, listing+delisting recorded), full Parquet/DuckDB lake, causal exhaustive sweep green (hard-gated) on real universe bars across a live + a delisting-tail symbol.
- **Compute:** M4a + M4b **LOCAL** (operator's ~660 GB disk; cloud only as a bandwidth fallback). **All intensive training from here is cloud CUDA A100/H100 — never MPS.**
- **De-risk:** M4a does all the design/validation for free so the paid M4b crossing is turnkey; the heavy data lift is isolated from modeling.

The metric implementations are instead validated by known-answer tests — every one of the 9 eval modules has hand-computed KATs.)*

A 7-agent adversarial review caught + fixed a **HIGH** lookahead (rollout μ̂ off-by-one → excluded the t→t+1 move so μ̂ matches the `log(C_{t+h}/C_{t+1})` label) and a cost-`σ̄` future-mean (→ causal). **Numbers are meaningless** (dev-grade, one symbol, synthetic Q3/Q4). *(This line used to end "Kronos metric cross-check deferred to M6 (weights load there anyway)". **The weights do not load there and never did** — the gate was dropped as binding on 2026-08-03 and `external_validation.GATE_IS_BINDING = False`. **M6 — The 2×2 + placebo ablation (THE experiment)** ✅ **CLOSED BY ITS OWN PRE-REGISTERED GATE — the designed experiment did not run, and that is the recorded outcome, not a failure to reach it.**
- **★ ITEM-E BRANCH, TAKEN 2026-08-12.** The micro-legibility gate fired on real data. §7 v1.5 **item E** — written **2026-07-30**, thirteen days earlier and before any of the refused data was seen — pre-committed the response in full: *gate fires → STOP; the primary becomes the MECHANISM FINDING.* Stage 2 was never entered for the microstructure arms; **artifacts_produced = 0** for cells 2–5. Cell 1 (BSQ + OHLCV-only, seeds 0/2/4) is the only arm with scored artifacts. The **once-only λ re-derivation the item permitted is SPENT**: λ ∈ {5, 8, 12} × 3 seeds × 2 arms, **0 of 18 clearing**, disposition FINAL — no fourth λ, ever (`runs_manifest/m6_lambda_sweep.json`).
- **What M6 produced instead, and it is the paper's primary:** the **eviction mechanism**, measured three ways that share no input — a synthetic fixture (return dim 0.98 / correlated filler dims 0.82–0.92 / an *independent* dim 0.001–0.014), a planted-information canary (**~1.15** nats in feature space → **zero** extracted — approximate as recorded in `docs/BUILD_RECORD.md`, no receipt holds a more exact value; **0.900** nats in token space → 94.4%, exact from `runs_manifest/m6_token_control_run_manifest.json`), and real data at 40 symbols × 150k rows/dim, **cell 4 seed 0** (TFI 0.8223, signed-count 0.7528 vs magnitude dims 0.8962–0.9320, with **97.3% of CELL 4's shortfall in the two SIGNED channels** — per-dim arithmetic in `docs/MODEL_CARD.md`; the 18 stored calibration replicates in `runs_manifest/m6_lambda_sweep.json` span 77–93%, so the direction is robust and the exact percentage is not — that file declares itself `NOT_COMPARABLE_TO_THE_GATE_FIRING_RUN`, so the spread is quoted as dispersion across replicates and **no delta against the 97.3% is a measured quantity**). ## The two binding M6 entry gates

**Gate A — Harness proven on the M3 checkpoint (vertical-slice discipline for eval).**
Before M6, the entire M5 harness must run end-to-end and leak-free on M3's single-symbol checkpoint: folds + Q1–Q4 + cost-aware net-IR + placebo + DSR/PBO all execute, and the §8.A.5/§8.E.5 causal-safety exhaustive sweep passes on a Q4 sample. The numbers are deliberately meaningless — this proves the *machine*, not the result. **No ablation until the harness is known-good.** (Prevents discovering a metric-code bug after 15 GPU-days.)

**Gate B — Microstructure kill-switch (pre-registered, bound to the M2 IC screen).**
- **Pre-registered threshold:** ≥1 microstructure channel must have (a) non-degenerate variance (not near-constant) **and** (b) a 95% moving-block-bootstrap RankIC CI **excluding 0** with **|RankIC| ≥ 2× the two-cell temporal MDE**, at a tradeable horizon `h ∈ {5, 15}`.
- **M2 verdict: PASS** — TFI |RankIC| 0.027 (7.1× the 0.0038 MDE) and signed_count_imbalance 0.020 (5.3×) @ h=5, both CIs exclude 0. (Magnitude channels fail; funding/OI N/A — masked in the single-symbol build, re-screened at M4 when the futures-API ingest lands.)
- **Branch on FAIL** (recorded for completeness): M6 does **not** spend GPU-days on the microstructure arms (Cells 2/4/5). M6 collapses to the **FSQ-vs-BSQ comparison only** (Cells 1 & 3, OHLCV-only), and the paper reframes to the honest finding *"trade-flow microstructure carries no tradable signal at 1m crypto."*
- **Branch on PASS (taken at entry, then SUPERSEDED at run time):** M6 was authorized to run all five cells — **but univariate liveness only justifies the spend; it does not prove the claim.** **What actually happened is neither branch of Gate B.** Gate B is an *entry* gate reading the M2 IC screen; it passed and the spend was authorized. A *second*, later gate — the micro-legibility gate, §7 v1.5 item E — fired on real data during the run and stopped it. So the microstructure claim was never decided by incremental net-IR at all: cells 2/4/5 have no scored artifacts. The thin |RankIC| ~0.02–0.03 did not vanish after costs; it never reached the money path, because the tokenizer did not make the channel legible to the AR. **That is the mechanism finding, and it is a stronger statement than either pre-written branch.**
